How we count

The same rules, written down and tested

Every number on your dashboard follows these rules. A test suite of hard billing cases runs on every release and fails if any MRR is off by one cent.

Annual plans

Count as one twelfth each month. Quarterly plans count as one third.

$1,200 a year = $100 MRR

Coupons

Lower MRR while they last. A 100% coupon counts as zero.

$100 plan, 50% off for 3 months = $50 MRR

Trials and one-time fees

Count as zero MRR. Trials show up in their own trial metrics.

$500 setup fee = $0 MRR, $500 other revenue

Past due

A customer more than 30 days past due counts as churned. Paying later counts as a reactivation.

Day 31 unpaid = churn, paid on day 40 = reactivation

Weekly plans

Count at 52 ÷ 12 = 4.33 weeks a month.

$30 a week = $130 MRR

Currencies

Turned into your currency at that day's rate. The rate used is stored with the number.

€92 on Nov 10 at 1.087 = $100.00

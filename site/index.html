How we count

The same rules, written down and tested

A test suite of hard billing cases runs on every release and fails if any MRR is off by one cent.

Annual plans

Count as one twelfth each month. Quarterly plans count as one third.

$1,200 a year = $100 MRR

Coupons

Lower MRR while they last. A 100% coupon counts as zero.

$100 plan at 50% off = $50 MRR

Trials and one-time fees

Count as zero MRR. Trials have their own metrics.

$500 setup fee = $0 MRR

Past due

More than 30 days past due counts as churn. Paying later counts as a reactivation.

Unpaid on day 31 = churn

Weekly plans

Count at 52 ÷ 12 = 4.33 weeks a month.

$30 a week = $130 MRR

Currencies

Turned into your currency at that day's rate, and the rate is stored with the number.

€92 at 1.087 = $100.00

Security

Built for trust from the first click

PulseARR reads your billing data and never moves money. Each team's data is fenced off from every other team.

  • Read-only access

    PulseARR can read Stripe and can't change it.

  • No card numbers

    The card page uses Stripe's own fields, so card numbers never reach our servers.

  • Delete any time

    Disconnecting a source or deleting your team removes the data for real.
